# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home buyers and renters searching for trustworthy local listings, property owners and builders publishing or managing listings, and administrators reviewing marketplace quality and safety. They often work on mobile or a laptop, switching quickly between discovery, response, and account tasks.

## Product Purpose

NokerBroker is a local property marketplace that helps people find, assess, publish, and safely manage real-estate listings. Success means users can act confidently: discover relevant homes, receive timely updates, manage inquiries, and understand account security without friction.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, approachable, and practical. The interface should feel familiar and calm rather than flashy, while communicating enough responsiveness that important marketplace and security events are never missed.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard spectacle, excessive glass effects, confusing custom controls, or visual changes that make the existing NokerBroker product feel like a different brand. Avoid animation that delays core tasks or reduces readability.

## Design Principles

- Preserve familiar workflows while making important state and next actions clearer.
- Put trust, safety, and accountability ahead of decoration.
- Make marketplace updates feel timely and actionable.
- Use motion only to explain state changes and improve orientation.
- Keep dense work, such as dashboards and review queues, readable on desktop and usable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-access requirements. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide clear focus states and semantic status messages, and never make color the only carrier of meaning.
